Concept

Expertise in every drop. Edge in every move.

An editorial brochure for Sundrex Oil Company Limited, designed to read like a premium industry magazine rather than a product catalogue. Big serif headlines, generous white space and justified columns carry the story; photography crosses the spine, slices into grids and opens into full-bleed moments.

The cover reduces the brand to one idea: a single ribbon of oil, flowing into an “S”, on black — with Sundrex Yellow as the only accent.
